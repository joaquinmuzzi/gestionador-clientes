# Code of Conduct

Gestionador de Clientes follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, reviews and any
other space related to it.

In short: be respectful and kind, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and focus on what is best for the project. Harassment, insults,
discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, tell the maintainer,
[@joaquinmuzzi](https://github.com/joaquinmuzzi), privately. Reports are handled privately and as
quickly as possible, and the person who reports is protected from retaliation.

The maintainer decides on the consequences following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant: from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
